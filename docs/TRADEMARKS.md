The following marks identify official releases, binaries, and container images

## 3. Permitted Uses (No Prior Permission Needed)

You do **not** need permission for truthful, non-misleading nominative use,
including:

- **Attribution & lineage:** Describing a fork or derivative as *"Built on Flux
  Engine"*, *"Forked from Flux Engine"*, *"Powered by Flux Engine"*, or
  *"Compatible with Flux Engine"*.
- **Unmodified redistribution:** Packaging or mirroring unmodified official
  releases, provided the original license notices, provenance metadata, and
  attribution remain intact.
- **Research, benchmarks & education:** Referencing Flux Engine by name in
  academic papers, blog posts, talks, tutorials, and reproducible benchmark
  comparisons (see [`CITATION.cff`](../CITATION.cff)).

---

## 4. Uses That Require Distinguishable Naming

To prevent confusion about release provenance, security signatures
([`SIGNING.md`](../SIGNING.md)), and support boundaries, please do **not**:

- Distribute a **modified** fork, custom binary, or third-party container image
  under the unmodified name **"Flux Engine"** or official Flux Engine logo in a
  way that implies it is an official release from `TheApathy/flux-engine`.
- Imply official endorsement, certification, or sponsorship by the Flux Engine
  maintainers for a third-party product, hosted service, or benchmark claim.

If you distribute a modified build or fork, choose a distinct project/image name
and note clearly that it is derived from Flux Engine (for example,
*"MyRuntime — a fork of Flux Engine"*).
